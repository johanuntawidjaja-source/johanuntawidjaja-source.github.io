Bali, beyond the familiar

Returning to Indonesia with an international cohort made familiar surroundings a subject of fresh questions.

My role

Participant · Konora team

Tools & approach

Entrepreneurship · Bilingual collaboration · Field visits

When / Status

May 2026
Two-week programme

01

Look again at what feels familiar.

The programme brought together entrepreneurship sessions, industry visits, and a team pitch. As an Indonesian studying in Singapore, I found it valuable to see how others understood Indonesia’s markets and opportunities. It challenged me to look beyond what I had taken for granted.

02

Translation was part of the teamwork.

In the Konora team, I helped bridge English and Indonesian discussions and presented the Business Model Canvas and closing slides. Sharing a language did not automatically mean sharing the same assumptions. Patience and changes in how I explained an idea helped the discussion move forward.

Explore the Konora proposal ↗
03

More time with the real operation.

In my reflection, I wanted longer industry visits and more time to understand how the businesses operated. The curriculum felt rushed, and slides were difficult to read from the back of the room. Sharing materials earlier and allowing deeper visits would have made the learning more effective.

04

Outside the classroom.

The weekend hike up Mount Batur was time spent with new friends beyond the group work. Those conversations and shared experiences were part of what made the trip valuable, alongside the formal programme.
