September 2026 · Music & leadership

Learning to rely on a team

My time as President of SUTD’s Chinese Orchestra, from March to September 2026, changed how I approached learning with others.

Start by asking

I used to try to learn everything on my own. Preparing for TENacity on the double bass, with guidance from my mentor Liew Soon How, showed me how much that approach left out. I was learning an instrument from scratch while preparing to perform.

Carry that lesson into leadership

As President, I learned to rely more on the executive committee, ask seniors for advice, and reach out when I needed help. Some performances went well; others needed more practice. A working team made the difference in how we approached both.

Still part of the orchestra

My presidency has ended, but I still perform with SUTDCO. The lesson I carry into engineering teams is straightforward: asking for help is part of doing the work well.
