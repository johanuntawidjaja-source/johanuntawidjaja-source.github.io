All work/SeenScreen

Product design

SeenScreen

A passenger-information concept that makes train crowd distribution visible before commuters choose where to board.

My role

Station, train & display CAD · Final design · Visual communication

Tools & approach

Fusion 360 · Product design · Presentation design

When / Status

2026
Team design concept & simulation

01

A clearer picture before boarding.

SeenScreen proposes a view of crowd distribution across an approaching MRT train. Passengers could use it to choose a less crowded carriage; station staff could use the same information when directing queues. These were intended uses of the concept, not outcomes established in a station trial.

02

My contribution, in context.

I modelled nearly the entire simulated system: the station, train, and display product. A teammate modelled the benches. I also produced the final product design, posters, and presentation slides, bringing the team’s concept into a consistent visual explanation.

03

The next design question.

A more complete evaluation would test whether passengers can understand the display quickly, where they notice it, and how the information changes their boarding decision. The CAD communicates the setting; it does not establish usability on its own.
