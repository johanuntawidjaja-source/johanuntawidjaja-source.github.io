Jurnal Lentera Bursa

A browser-local trading journal and calculator for Indonesian investors, inspired by my mother’s handwritten trading records.

My role

Independent build · AI as my teammate

Tools & approach

Web development · Software testing · Cloudflare

When / Status

September 2026
Working web application

01

A familiar problem.

My mother kept track of her stocks across notebooks and loose sheets. I wanted to bring the record and the calculations together: positions, watchlist assumptions, fees, break-even prices, and Indonesia Stock Exchange trading limits.

02

The work behind the interface.

I built the product with AI as my teammate during “How to Build AI Products – and Prove They Work”, led by S. Anand. I defined requirements and checked the implementation through tests and observed behaviour. Journal entries stay in the user’s browser; the gated deployment adds verified access and filtered operational telemetry.

After an early deployment included files that should not have been public, the deployment was restricted to a dedicated public directory.

03

Evidence, with boundaries.

The current suite contains 52 checks covering access verification and telemetry filtering. Four authenticated testers completed 17 monitored sessions and 250 interaction events, with no JavaScript exceptions recorded during that window. These are early test results, not evidence of broad adoption or an independent security audit.
